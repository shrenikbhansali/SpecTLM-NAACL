# P6 — A40 serving-time pilot, D-48

Completed 2026-10-08T16:16:15.192824-04:00. Status: **pilot**; all 30 planned timing cells finished. Acceptance statistics are reported separately from the frozen harness.

Five arms use the same R1-Distill-Llama target and derivative-rendered prompt IDs: reused family EAGLE-3, fc-only repair, full repair, dedicated oracle, and no speculation. Repair exports are the original self-elicited 256-example / 300-step seed-0 checkpoints. Batch 8 uses SPEED-128; batch 1 uses the fixed, hash-selected32-query subset. Each condition has three fresh A40 processes, each with one first (cold) panel and three warm panels. Prefix caching is disabled.

All runs use vLLM 0.31.0, greedy decoding, seed 0 and at most 512 generated tokens. The timing extension 215fb44 imports and hashes frozen 6da2e42 prompt validation and uses its engine settings. It produces timing records, not acceptance numbers. Cold means the first panel **after** vLLM startup/compilation warmup; startup is measured separately. No history or frozen engine code changed.

Intervals use 10,000 paired process/prompt-batch bootstrap draws; the three warm passes are averaged within a process. Batch time is counted once, rather than copied onto eight prompts. These are three process replicates on available A40s, not an independently randomized sample of exclusive hosts.

**Conditional repair amortization.** The exact timed seed-0 repair costs, including successful query/response generation, are 0.465 A40 GPU-hours for fc and 0.452 for full. Shared data generation is charged separately to each alternative. These observed elapsed costs have no training-cost confidence interval; they do not establish that full is intrinsically cheaper. Search, engineering, evaluation and failed jobs are excluded from this per-chosen-repair cost. Dedicated-drafter training cost is unavailable and is not invented.
