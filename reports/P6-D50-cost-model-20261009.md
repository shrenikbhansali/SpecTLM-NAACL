Limitations:
- Pilot empirical fixed-K4 fit; K coefficient is unidentified; no K6/K8 conversion
- Fit on earlier self256 and self4k timings only; 16k timings never influence fit
- 2000 joint process/prompt-batch bootstrap draws; warm repeats averaged within process; CI conditional on model form, not model misspecification
- SPEED32 batch1 tau uses the same 32 queries but frozen acceptance was measured at batch8; output/scheduling differences remain
- Different A40 hosts and background loads; not randomized exclusive placement
- Macro tau differs from token-weighted effective tau; output lengths and scheduling may cause poor cost prediction; validation residuals reported
- Applies only to R1, production EAGLE3 architecture and SPEED; MATH, Nemotron, official/independent drafters need separate timing calibration
- Dedicated oracle has only one acceptance condition; its tau-dependent slope cannot be independently identified; report measured timings
- Predictions are not measured speedups; extrapolated tau flagged; no-spec controls and all measured arms may have different greedy outputs
