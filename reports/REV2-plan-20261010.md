## 4. Writing changes (no experiments needed; claude-ops applies them during integration)
1. Mechanism framing (abstract, §1, §3, conclusion): post-training changes the text a model writes, the hidden states the drafter reads and the tokens it must predict. ReFit learns all three from the derivative. Add the crossover-on-parent-text result and the reasoning-text controls as the evidence that the model, not the genre, moves. Drop "teach it to read again" as the sole mechanism.
2. Terminology: use "teacher-distilled" / "distillation-heavy post-training" consistently. Describe R1-Distill as distilled into Llama-3.1-8B (base) and cite base-model retention (122%). Use the Tülu ladder as matched-checkpoint evidence.
3. Compute claim: replace ">400×" with "more than two orders of magnitude", stated as a recipe estimate that includes data generation.
4. Present ReFit-full as the recommended method and ReFit-interface as the lightweight, shareable variant (shared drafter body, ~100 MB per derivative).
5. §6.4: "Across all seven settings the pattern of Fig. 2 holds" → "in all seven settings ReFit-interface alone delivers most of ReFit-full's gain" (true: 57–90%).
6. Fig. 1 caption: census uses the production family drafters of Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct and Qwen3-8B; Table 1 uses the EAGLE authors' drafter.
7. §3: the component analysis uses the production drafter (consistent with the appendix). Fig. 2 "2.3×" matches 21.9/9.6; say "more than twice" in text.
8. §6.1: list speedups in variant order (1.74× ReFit-interface, 1.83× ReFit-full).
9. §2: justify p1 as the drafter's next-token accuracy, independent of how acceptance compounds across draft positions, not "independent of response length".
10. Abstract: speedups are measured on SPEED-Bench (and on MATH if E10b lands); acceptance on MATH-500.
11. Licensing: add self-elicited prompts as the license-free option (A10).
12. Specify the reduced-vocabulary KL (A5).
13. Related work: EDA adds gated shared/private experts that change the drafter architecture; ReFit keeps the released architecture so it loads in unchanged engines.
14. Release statement (owner decision): code, per-model census results, repaired drafters.
15. Title (owner decision): optionally narrow to "...for Distilled Reasoning Models".
